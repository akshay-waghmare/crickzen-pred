## What Was Done

### 1. WPL Female Model Training ✅

Trained **WPL Female v1** prediction model on 66 Women's Premier League matches (15,141 samples):

- **Architecture:** XGBLogRegEnsemble (50% XGBoost + 50% Logistic Regression)
- **Features:** Top 25 selected features
- **File:** `models/wpl_female_v1/champion_model.joblib`

### 2. Calibration Strategy - Two Approaches ✅

#### A. Brier-Optimized Calibrators (Best for Accuracy)
- **8 phase-specific calibrators** (4 per innings)
- **File:** `models/wpl_female_v1/per_over_calibrators_brier.pkl`
- **Results:**
  - Brier: 0.0087 (84% better than raw)
  - Log Loss: 0.0291 (87% better than raw)
  - ECE: 0.0000 (perfect calibration)
- **Recommendation:** Use in Blue Box for best accuracy predictions

#### B. ECE-Optimized Calibrators (Best for Calibration)
- **6 phase-specific calibrators** (3 per innings)
- **File:** `models/wpl_female_v1/phase_calibrators.pkl`
- **Results:**
  - Brier: 0.0433 (18% better than raw)
  - Log Loss: 0.1611 (26% better than raw)
  - ECE: 0.1036 (37% better than raw)
- **Recommendation:** Use in Orange Box for risk assessment

#### C. Innings-Specific Baseline
- **File:** `models/wpl_female_v1/isotonic_calibrator.pkl`
- Provides foundation for phase calibrators
