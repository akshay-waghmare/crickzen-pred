1. Executive Summary

⚠️ Key Finding: IPL v14 calibration hurts in high-chase innings-2 situations. The production calibrated model overpredicts win probability by 8–17% in the 10–30% probability buckets for high-chase MID and DEATH phases. Raw predictions are statistically better in these segments.
